# Beyond the course: comparison with the baseline build

The two freeCodeCamp courses by Radu Mariescu-Istodor ("Self-Driving Car with JavaScript (No
Libraries)" and "Build a Virtual World Filled with Self-Driving Cars") end with a working build
that is the floor for this project, not the target. Each row names one limit of that build, what
NeuroDrive City does instead, and where the evidence lives (unit tests in `tests/`, end-to-end
specs in `e2e/`, source files in `src/`).

| # | Course build (baseline) | NeuroDrive City | Evidence |
| - | ----------------------- | --------------- | -------- |
| 1 | Cars ignore every marking: stop, yield, crossing, parking, light and target are decorative. | Every marking changes behaviour: traffic stops at stop lines (full stop, then at least 1 s), yields to conflicts, crawls over crossings, stops for red and for yellow when it can, parks in bays, spawns at starts and drives to targets (D1 to D6). | `tests/engine/markings/behaviour.test.ts` (stop state machine, yield, crossing, parking, start, target, light helpers), `tests/engine/sim/simulation.test.ts` ("traffic routes to random targets, obeys markings and stays under 5% damaged over 60 s"), `src/engine/markings/*` |
| 2 | No pathfinding or target seeking. | Lane-level A* over directed lane edges (no U-turns except at dead ends), lane paths with kerb arcs at turns, pure-pursuit steering with braking for curves, cars ahead, markings and the end of the route (F1 to F4). The AI cars also learn to drive a route themselves, in the right-hand lane (v1.1): five route inputs from the lane path, lane-discipline fitness (progress counts only in lane, more than 1 s over the centre line eliminates the car) and a route curriculum. The bundled city brain drives Grid Town's 555 m route with nine corners from Start to Target in about 36 s, 99 to 100% of the time in lane, in 4 of 5 validation drives (5 of 20 on unseen traffic; traffic queues are its weak point). | `tests/engine/nav/nav.test.ts` (A* shortest path, one-way respected, route follower reaches the target in an L-shaped world), `tests/engine/ai/lane.test.ts` (lane credit, centre-line elimination, route inputs), `tests/engine/ai/curriculum.test.ts`, `tests/engine/ai/pretrained.test.ts` ("drives Grid Town from the Start to the Target in its lane without a crash"), `e2e/sim2d.spec.ts` (the demo car stays right of the centre line), `e2e/training.spec.ts` (Train mode keeps the best car at least 60% in lane), `src/engine/nav/*`, `src/engine/ai/fitness.ts` |
| 3 | Markings are not attached to roads and orphan when a segment is deleted. | Markings are bound to a segment, a parameter and a side; they follow moves, are removed with their segment and re-bind to the correct half when a segment is split (B5). | `tests/engine/markings/marking.test.ts` ("follows the segment when its points move", "is removed with its segment (never orphaned)", "re-binds to the correct half on split") |
| 4 | Editing: no undo, no segment selection, split or delete, no multi-select, no shortcuts, no touch, middle-button-only pan, zoom out only and around the canvas centre. | 200-step undo and redo for every command, segment selection, Alt+click split, Delete, Shift+drag box select, a full shortcut table, touch pan and pinch zoom, middle, right or Space+drag pan, eased zoom toward the cursor in both directions (B1 to B9). | `tests/editor/history.test.ts` (every command round trips), `tests/render2d/viewport.test.ts`, `e2e/editor.spec.ts`, `src/app/keymap.ts` |
| 5 | Any edit regenerates the whole world synchronously, detected by a per-frame JSON hash; the 472-segment sample town freezes the tab on every drag and on OSM import. | A version counter replaces hashing; unions are bounding-box prefiltered; dragging shows a cheap preview and regenerates on release; worlds over 400 segments generate in a Web Worker with a progress bar; OSM import is chunked across frames (C7, J5). | `e2e/world.spec.ts` (drag in a 325-segment world averages 16.7 ms per frame; a 580-segment world generates in a worker), `tests/engine/math/polygon.test.ts` ("unions a 500-polygon road grid well under a second"), `src/app/worldManager.ts` |
| 6 | Geometry hacks: ray-cast origin fixed at (-1000, -1000), midpoint-only containment, point-sampled polygon distance, zero-length segments after splitting, and a union that mutates its inputs. | Exact predicates: point in polygon with a horizontal ray that handles vertices, segment classification that is not fooled by midpoints, true segment-to-segment distance, splitting that never produces zero-length pieces, and a union that never mutates its inputs (A3). | `tests/engine/math/polygon.test.ts` ("counts a ray through a vertex correctly", "is not fooled by a segment whose midpoint is inside but ends outside", "distance uses true segment-to-segment distance", "breakAtIntersections never mutates inputs and never makes zero-length pieces") |
| 7 | Unseeded randomness: trees jump on every regenerate. | One seeded generator (mulberry32) for everything in the engine; the engine may not call `Math.random` (lint rule). The same graph, settings and seed always give the same city (A1, C6). | `tests/engine/math/rng.test.ts`, `tests/engine/world/world.test.ts` (determinism snapshot), `eslint.config.js` (engine restrictions) |
| 8 | Traffic lights use frame-count timing, have no all-red phase, and crash the loop when no junction exists. | Controllers in simulation seconds (green 6 s, yellow 1.5 s, all-red 0.5 s) with exactly one green approach per junction, fading lamps, robust to large time steps; a light with no junction forms its own controller (D5). | `tests/engine/markings/behaviour.test.ts` ("keeps exactly one approach green", "cycles green 6 s → yellow 1.5 s → all-red 0.5 s", "a light with no junction nearby forms its own controller, never throws") |
| 9 | The save format stores derived geometry (6.37 MB for 472 segments), overflows localStorage so autosave fails silently, and loading reloads the page. | Files store only the graph, markings, settings, seed and view (the 92-road sample town with its OSM features is 9.7 KB); autosave goes to IndexedDB with a world library and thumbnails; loading never reloads (J1, J2). | `tests/engine/io/serialize.test.ts` ("stores only source data, so files stay small", "save → load → regenerate gives identical derived counts"), `e2e/editor.spec.ts` (save, reload, state persists), `e2e/osm.spec.ts` (world library) |
| 10 | OSM: `oneway="no"` is treated as one-way, ways without tags crash, and there is no highway filtering. | `no` is two-way, `-1` reverses, roundabouts are one-way, untagged ways are skipped, only drivable highway types are imported, bridges and tunnels are carried, and building footprints and water are imported as fixed features (J4, J6). | `tests/engine/osm/osm.test.ts` ("interprets oneway values correctly", "skips untagged and non-drivable ways, carries bridge/tunnel, collects buildings and water"), `tests/engine/world/features.test.ts` |
| 11 | The minimap shows only graph lines and a dot. | The minimap draws roads, buildings, water and trees from a cached layer, cars by kind, the pulsing best car and targets, the camera footprint (a frustum in 3D), and click or drag moves the camera (I1 to I3). | `src/render2d/minimap.ts`, `e2e/world.spec.ts` (static layer redrawn only on world changes) |
| 12 | Simulation: no traffic, no car-to-car collisions, damaged cars still compute, the camera is locked to the best car, one generation per manual save plus a page reload, and no generation counter, fitness display, stuck timeout, seeds or crossover. | Route-following traffic, car-to-car collisions, damaged cars stop computing, chase, orbit, top and cinematic cameras plus click-to-follow, automatic generations without reloads, a generation counter, live fitness and a sparkline, stuck elimination, seeded generations, tournament selection with crossover, elitism, turbo training in a worker and a validated pre-trained brain (E3, F4, G4 to G8, K9). | `tests/engine/ai/ai.test.ts` (evolution, crossover, elitism, gen 10 beats gen 1), `tests/engine/ai/pretrained.test.ts`, `tests/render3d/cameraRig.test.ts`, `e2e/training.spec.ts` |
| 13 | Rendering redraws everything every frame without frustum culling or layer caching, draws cars under buildings in the fake-3D view, and runs about 1.7 M intersection tests per frame at 100 cars. | Spatial hashes for sensors and damage (300 cars step in 1.6 ms), cached static layers, frustum culling, a pseudo-3D painter's sort that includes cars, and instanced 3D (77 draw calls for the demo city with shadows and bloom) (A5, I3, L4, K3 to K5). | `tests/engine/sim/simulation.test.ts` ("steps 300 sensing cars in well under 4 ms"), `tests/engine/graph/spatialHash.test.ts`, `e2e/sim3d.spec.ts` (draw calls at most 150, GPU memory back to baseline), `e2e/fps.spec.ts` |
| 14 | Global-scope scripts with no modules, types, tests, lint, build or CI. | Strict TypeScript ES modules, Vitest unit tests (engine coverage above 80%), Playwright end-to-end tests against the production build, ESLint with type-aware rules, Prettier, and GitHub Actions for CI and Pages deploys. | `package.json` scripts, `tsconfig*.json`, `eslint.config.js`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` |
